## Pricing factors

**Heading:** Pricing factors

**Text:** The cost of warehousing services depends on:

- **Storage area** — square metres or pallet spaces
- **Storage period** — the longer the period, the more favourable the daily rate
- **Cargo type** — standard, temperature-controlled, hazardous or fragile
- **Additional services** — loading/unloading, packing and labelling
- **Warehouse location** — warehouses in large cities may cost more

## Working example

**Heading:** Working example

**Case:** Consolidation of goods from Germany for an online store

**Cargo:** 5 consignments from different suppliers, 45 pallets, 8,500 kg

**Solution:**
- Collection of cargo at a warehouse in Wrocław, Poland
- Consolidation into one shipment
- FTL transport to Kyiv

**Timeframe:** 5 days (collection + consolidation + delivery)

**Result:** The client received all goods in one shipment and saved 35% on delivery costs.
