## Pricing factors

**Heading:** Pricing factors

**Text:** The cost of international transport depends on:

- **Cargo type** — standard, temperature-controlled, hazardous (ADR) or oversized
- **Weight and volume** — affect vehicle selection and the amount of cargo space required
- **Route** — distance, number of borders and customs checkpoints
- **Urgency** — standard or express delivery
- **Additional services** — customs clearance, insurance, loading/unloading
- **Seasonality** — peak periods (October–December) may be more expensive

- Phytosanitary certificates for food products

## FAQ

**Heading:** Frequently asked questions

1. **How can I calculate the transport cost?**
   - The price depends on weight, volume, distance and cargo type. Send us an enquiry and we will prepare a quote within the day.

2. **Which documents are required for customs clearance?**
   - An invoice, packing list, contract and, where necessary, certificate of origin. We will provide the complete list after reviewing your cargo.

3. **Can I track my cargo during transport?**
   - Yes. We provide a tracking number and update statuses at every delivery stage.

4. **How long does delivery from Poland take?**
   - Standard delivery takes 3–4 days for LTL and 2–3 days for FTL.

5. **Do you transport hazardous goods?**
   - Yes. We arrange and deliver ADR-class cargo in compliance with all safety requirements.

6. **How does payment work?**
   - Payment is made by invoice to a bank account. Prepayment or post-payment may be available by agreement.
