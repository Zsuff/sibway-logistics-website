## Common customs clearance issues

**Heading:** Common customs clearance issues

### Issue: Incorrect UKTZED code

**Consequence:** Delay, penalty or recalculation of customs payments

**Solution:** Our brokers verify the code before the declaration is submitted.

### Issue: Missing certificates

**Consequence:** The cargo cannot be cleared at the border.

**Solution:** We help obtain the required certificates in advance.

### Issue: Delay at a customs checkpoint

**Consequence:** Vehicle downtime and additional costs

**Solution:** We accompany the cargo and resolve issues promptly on site.

## Customs clearance example

**Heading:** Customs clearance example

**Cargo:** production equipment, Germany → Ukraine

**UKTZED code:** 8462 99 90 00 (metalworking machine tools)

**Customs value:** €45,000

**Customs payments:**
- Customs duty: 0.2% = €90
- VAT: 20% = €9,000
- Excise duty: 0% (non-excise goods)

**Clearance time:** 2 days

**Documents:**
- Invoice
- Packing list
- Sale and purchase agreement
- Certificate of conformity
- CMR consignment note

**Result:** The cargo was customs-cleared without delays, and the client received a full set of closing documents.

## FAQ

**Heading:** Frequently asked questions

1. **Which documents are required for customs clearance?**
   - An invoice, packing list, contract and, where necessary, certificate of origin. We provide the full list after reviewing your cargo.

2. **How long does customs clearance take?**
   - The standard timeframe is 1–3 days for imports and 1–2 days for exports. Expedited clearance takes one day.

3. **Do you help with goods classification?**
   - Yes. Our brokers determine the UKTZED code and verify it before submitting the declaration.

4. **Which customs payments apply?**
   - Customs duty, VAT and, where applicable, excise duty. Rates depend on the UKTZED code and country of origin.

5. **Do you work with private individuals?**
   - Yes. We support customs clearance for individuals importing goods.

6. **How does payment for services work?**
   - Payment is made by invoice to a bank account. Prepayment or post-payment may be available by agreement.
