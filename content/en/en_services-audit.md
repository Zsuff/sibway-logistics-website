## Working example

**Heading:** Working example

**Case:** Logistics audit for an electronics distributor

**Client:** A Kyiv-based distributor of electronics and household appliances

**Challenge:** High logistics costs, delivery delays and an unclear cost structure

**Solution:**
- Analysed six months of logistics data
- Identified three key problems: inefficient routes, lack of consolidation and customs delays
- Proposed route optimisation, cargo consolidation and changes to logistics partners

**Result:**
- 28% reduction in costs
- Delivery times reduced by 2 days
- Improved transparency of logistics processes

   - A standard audit takes 7–14 days, depending on the size of the business and logistics complexity.
