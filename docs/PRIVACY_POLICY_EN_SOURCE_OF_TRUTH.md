## 1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy describes how Private Entrepreneur HALAHUZ SERHII, Taxpayer Identification Number 3179115935, registered address: 26 Makarova Street, Apt. 13, Rivne, Rivne Oblast, 33024, Ukraine, hereinafter referred to as the "Company", processes the personal data of website visitors and persons who contact the Company regarding international freight transportation.

The website is intended for a B2B audience. This Policy applies to the data you provide through website forms or during further communication regarding a specific enquiry.

For questions regarding the processing of personal data, please contact:
[sibway@ukr.net](mailto:sibway@ukr.net).

## 2. What Data We May Process and How We Obtain It

Depending on how you contact us, the Company may process:

- your name;
- your telephone number;
- your email address, if you provide it;
- your company name, if you provide it;
- information you voluntarily provide in the description of your enquiry, cargo, route, or transportation conditions;
- correspondence and communication data related to your enquiry;
- technical data related to the use of the website, including data that may be collected through web analytics tools and cookies.

We obtain data directly from you when you:

- submit a form on the website;
- contact us by email;
- continue communication through an agreed communication channel;
- provide documents or data required to prepare for or carry out transportation.

We do not collect your company registration number or registered address through the website form. Such data may be provided only at later stages of cooperation, including when submitting an application, agreeing the terms, or entering into a contract.

## 3. Purpose of Data Processing

The Company may use data to:

- process your enquiry;
- prepare a transportation quotation;
- clarify cargo details, the route, and transportation conditions;
- prepare a commercial offer;
- communicate regarding specific transportation;
- enter into, perform, and support contracts;
- comply with legal requirements and maintain the necessary records;
- analyse website traffic and traffic sources.

We do not use data received through the website form for advertising or marketing mailings.

## 4. Parties to Whom Data May Be Disclosed

For the operation of the website and the processing of enquiries, data may be processed by technical providers necessary for the functioning of the form, delivery of enquiries, website hosting, email, and web analytics.

Data may be disclosed to partners necessary for organising and carrying out specific transportation, to the extent necessary to fulfil the relevant enquiry, application, agreed offer, or contract.

The Company does not sell or disclose personal data for the independent marketing purposes of third parties.

## 5. Cookies and Web Analytics

The website may use cookies and web analytics tools to analyse website traffic and traffic sources. This helps us evaluate the website's performance and improve its functionality.

Detailed information about the use of cookies may be provided in an informational notice on the website.

### 5.1. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

## 6. Data Retention Periods

Enquiries that do not result in contractual relations are retained for up to 12 months from the date of the last interaction with you, unless otherwise required to protect the Company's rights and legitimate interests or required by law.

Data related to an entered-into contract may be retained for periods necessary to perform the contract, maintain accounting and other mandatory records, and protect the Company's rights.

## 7. Your Rights

You may contact the Company with questions regarding the processing of your personal data and the exercise of rights provided for by applicable law, including access, updating, correction, restriction of processing, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, where applicable.

We may be unable to fulfil a request in whole or in part where the retention or processing of data is necessary to comply with legal requirements, perform a contract, or protect the Company's rights.

To exercise your rights, please write to
[sibway@ukr.net](mailto:sibway@ukr.net).

## 8. Data Security

The Company applies organisational and technical measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting data over the internet or storing data electronically can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Depending on technical providers and the communication channels selected, the processing of personal data may involve transfers of data outside Ukraine. Where such transfers occur, the Company seeks to ensure an appropriate level of data protection in accordance with applicable requirements.
